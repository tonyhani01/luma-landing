About LUMA

Built and operated in Cairo, Egypt · Last updated: September 17, 2026

LUMA is an AI sales and customer-service teammate for Shopify merchants in Egypt and the Middle East. It answers Instagram DMs and Shopify storefront chat in Arabic, English, and Franco-Arabic — using the merchant's own store data: real prices, real stock, real order status. When the data doesn't clearly support an answer, LUMA stops and hands the conversation to a human.

Why LUMA exists

In Egypt and the Gulf, customers ask their questions where they already are — in the DMs: «بكام؟», "3andoko size L?", «فين الأوردر؟». Those messages arrive around the clock, and most merchants answer them by hand, one by one.

At LUMA's first live store, nearly half of all customer questions arrived after hours (8 pm – 9 am), and high-intent buyers rarely wait until morning. LUMA exists to close that gap: answer from the store's own data, in the customer's own language, at any hour — and stay out of the way when a human should step in.

How we work

  • Answers come from real data. Every price, size, and order status LUMA quotes is read from the merchant's Shopify store — nothing is invented.
  • The customer's language, message by message. Arabic script, Franco-Arabic, and English, including customers who switch scripts mid-thread.
  • It knows when to stop. LUMA scores its own confidence on every reply. When the catalog, policy, or order data doesn't support an answer, it hands the conversation to the merchant's team with the full thread attached.
  • Merchant data stays merchant data. Customer conversations and store data are used only to answer customers — never sold, never used for advertising, and deleted 30 days after the app is uninstalled.
