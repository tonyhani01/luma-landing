FAQ

How long does setup take?

Minutes for store chat. Add Instagram and you’re live the same day.

Which stores can use LUMA?

Any Shopify store. Instagram DMs need an Instagram Business or Creator account.

Does it really understand Franco?

Yes. Franco and Egyptian Arabic are core, alongside English.

Where do prices and stock come from?

Straight from your Shopify catalog, inventory, policies and orders. Always current.

What if a question is tricky?

LUMA passes it to your team with the full thread, so nobody has to ask the customer twice.

Can my team reply from LUMA?

Yes. One shared inbox: claim a chat, reply, or pause LUMA on that thread.

What happens to customer data?

It’s used only to answer your customers. Details in the Privacy Policy.

How are conversations counted?

One customer chat LUMA handles that day, across Instagram and store chat combined.
